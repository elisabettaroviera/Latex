%-----------------------------------------------------------
\section{Introduction}
%-----------------------------------------------------------

This chapter evaluates the predictive robustness, cross-cohort transferability,
and biological coherence of the CpG signatures identified in
Chapter~\ref{chap:feature_selection}.  
The objective is threefold: (i) assess intra-dataset classification
performance, (ii) quantify inter-dataset generalisation,
and (iii) investigate whether a compressed subset of CpGs
retains both predictive and biological relevance.
